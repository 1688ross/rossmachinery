# Questions for the office meeting (2026-10-20)

Short list. Each one closes a loose end in the build; the line under it says what the answer decides.

## Companies, contacts, divisions

1. **At Sikorsky (and anyone else with divisions): do the divisions you each sell to have their own PO numbering, their own bill-to address, or their own payment terms? Or is it one Sikorsky on paper with different people in different buildings?**
   Decides whether a division is a tag on the contact (one company on the list) or its own entry on the company list.

2. **Who owns the company and contact lists? Can anyone add or correct a company or a person, and who cleans up a duplicate?**
   Today anyone who can write can add. The list only stays useful if someone is allowed to merge and retire entries.

3. **When a quote goes to several people at one company, do they all get the same PDF, and who is the "Dear" line?**
   The build addresses the first person ticked and lists the rest. Confirm that is how you want it.

4. **Order types: Custom, OTR Machine, Parts, Materials, Accessories, Software, Service/Maintenance, Repairs, Misc. One type per order?** Where does a machine sold with accessories and installation land?
   The type is the headline for reporting; the line items underneath keep their own categories.

5. **For reports by salesperson, whose order is it: the person who quoted it, the person who placed the PO, or can it be split?**
   Decides the one field that commission-by-person reports hang on.

## QuickBooks (Desktop)

The one question underneath all of these: for each kind of record, which system is the master, and at what moment does it cross over.

6. **Customer invoices: is the invoice made in QuickBooks first and the folder records it, or should the folder make the invoice and send it to QuickBooks?**
   Same question for vendor bills. Decides the direction of the connector and who types a number once.

7. **Payments and remittances: they are entered in QuickBooks today. Should the platform read them from QuickBooks so a folder's "customer paid" light turns green on its own, instead of someone typing the payment twice?**
   This is the single biggest time saver on offer and the lowest-risk integration (read-only).

8. **Customers and vendors: QuickBooks already has the lists. Load the platform's company list from QuickBooks once, then which side is the master when a name or address changes?**
   Avoids two lists drifting apart.

9. **How is commission recorded in QuickBooks today (an income account, a class, a job per deal)?**
   Decides whether net sales and commission reports from the platform can tie out to the books to the penny, or only approximately.

10. **Which machine runs QuickBooks Desktop, who signs into it, and is it on all day?**
    The connector has to run on a Windows machine with QuickBooks and the company file open. If that is Tammy's PC, the sync only runs when her PC is on.

11. **What must never post to the books without a person approving it?**
    Recommendation to put on the table: the platform prepares and suggests; Tammy approves in one click; nothing reaches QuickBooks automatically.
