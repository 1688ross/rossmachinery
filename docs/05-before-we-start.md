# Before we start

Date: 2026-09-29. One page. Everything else we figure out as we go, by putting real screens in front of the
office and adjusting.

## The rule

No two orders are the same, and the system is built for that. Anything that actually happens can be recorded
as it happened: any number of invoices, payments, credits or vendor bills on a folder; a check that covers
several folders; freight that is marked up, passed through, or absorbed; a client paid in full for something
that was later dropped. Nothing has to be decided in advance for those to work. The screens will let you
write down what happened, and nothing will be silently lost.

Where the software needs a starting point, it uses one, and every starting point is changeable later.

## How we learn the rest

- Build the core first: folders, the money ledger, documents, the pile of what needs attention.
- Put it in front of your dad and the office manager with real open folders as early as possible.
- Watch what they do, what they type in the notes, and what still sends them to the paper. Change the screens
  to match. Repeat.
