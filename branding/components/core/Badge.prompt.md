Compact pill label for status, category, or a machine's speciality tag.

```jsx
<Badge tone="yellow" variant="soft">5 Axis Turn Mills</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" variant="solid">In Stock</Badge>
```

Tones: `navy`, `blue` (default), `yellow`, `neutral`, `success`, `danger`. Variants: `soft` (tinted, default), `solid`, `outline`.
